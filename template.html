How to read this chart

Each dot is one completed issue, placed at the date it reached a Done status; its height is the cycle time, meaning how long it spent between the selected clock-start point and completion. The shaded band covers one standard deviation either side of the mean for the current slice: a tight band means predictable delivery. Dots above the dashed 85th-percentile line are the slowest 15% and are worth a look. Issues that jumped from To Do straight to Done, never entering an in-progress status, have no meaningful cycle time and are excluded by default; the "Straight to Done" filter brings them back, clocked from their creation date. Time is counted in working days: Saturdays and Sundays are subtracted from every measurement, so a ticket picked up on Friday afternoon and finished Monday morning counts well under a day rather than three. Switch "Day count" to Calendar for raw elapsed time. Public holidays are not excluded. Click any dot to open the issue in Jira.
